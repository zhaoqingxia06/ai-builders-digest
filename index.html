The takeaway: app companies are becoming the hottest AI labs, and the real business is the bridge between model capability and the messy workflows enterprises already have.

Box CEO Aaron Levie's core judgment on Sequoia's Training Data podcast — the once-mocked "model wrapper" is now the most defensible business, because what enterprises buy is not raw model capability but the bridge from that capability to their existing workflows.
