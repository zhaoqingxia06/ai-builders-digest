The takeaway: once AI agents start spending their own money, card rails (a 30¢ flat fee per transaction) become the ceiling — stablecoins plus self-custodial wallets are the ready-made low-cost global settlement layer, and Coinbase is going all in on it.

Inside Coinbase engineering, every team, repository, and even individual gets a "brain" — the full history of incidents, financial controls, A/B tests, and accepted or rejected PRs. Using the internal harness Toshi, he now breaks a feature plan into phases, spins up 10 parallel agents (mostly cheaper models) to execute, and gets a completion ping minutes later — the CEO is shipping features himself again.
